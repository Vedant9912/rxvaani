Scene 1 • 0:00 - 0:18 Introduction & Vision

"Welcome to RxVaani, an intelligent clinical prescription scribe engineered specifically for the Indian healthcare ecosystem, developed for the Great Indian AI Internship Challenge powered by Gnani AI. Our mission is to transform ambient doctor-patient consultations into fully structured, verified digital prescriptions in under ninety seconds."

Scene 2 • 0:18 - 0:38 The OPD Dilemma

"In busy Indian outpatient departments, a doctor examines up to eighty patients every single day. Doctors spend over forty percent of their consultation time writing or typing prescriptions. Illegible handwriting frequently causes dangerous dispensing errors in pharmacies, while existing Western voice tools fail completely on Indian code-mixed speech."

Scene 3 • 0:38 - 0:58 Ambient Audio Intake

"RxVaani solves this seamlessly. It captures ambient doctor-patient conversations through clinic microphones or telephonic audio streams. Patients and doctors speak naturally in Hindi and Hinglish, expressing symptoms, duration, and medication plans without altering their natural clinical dialogue."

Scene 4 • 0:58 - 1:18 Gnani AI Prisma STT

"At the deep-tech core of RxVaani is Gnani AI's state-of-the-art Prisma Speech-to-Text engine. Utilizing the Prisma version 3 REST API in codemix mode, it flawlessly transcribes code-mixed Hindi and English, accurately preserving pharmaceutical brand names and numerical dosages with sub-four-hundred millisecond response times."

Scene 5 • 1:18 - 1:40 Clinical Fuzzy Matching

"RxVaani's clinical parsing engine extracts structured medical entities: chief complaints, vitals, diagnosis, and medications. To protect against speech recognition errors, our fuzzy matching algorithm cross-references phonetically similar brand names against a curated formulary of over fifty common Indian pharmaceuticals using Levenshtein distance matching."

Scene 6 • 1:40 - 2:00 Safety & Doctor Review

"Patient safety is non-negotiable. RxVaani adheres strictly to the 'AI Drafts, Doctor Decides' paradigm. The system never issues autonomous prescriptions. Amber warning badges flag unverified dosages or low confidence scores. Doctors retain total agency to review, edit, and digitally authorize every line item."

Scene 7 • 2:00 - 2:20 NMC PDF Export & Architecture

"With one click, an official, print-ready digital prescription PDF is generated adhering to National Medical Commission standards, complete with clinic branding, registration numbers, structured medicines table, and legal disclaimer. Built with a robust FastAPI backend and enterprise Java Spring Boot option, RxVaani is production-ready for India's clinical future."
